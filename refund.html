Our 48-Hour Money-Back Guarantee

We stand behind the quality of our signals. If you are not satisfied for any reason within 48 hours of your purchase, we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund within the 48-hour window: message @titalsignals on Telegram or email support@titalsignals.com with your order details. We will process your refund within 24 hours of receiving your request.

Refund Processing Time

Once approved, refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time: Credit/debit cards: 5–10 business days. Cryptocurrency: 1–3 business days (at current market rate). Apple Pay / Google Pay: 3–5 business days.

Monthly Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Cancellation stops future billing. We do not provide prorated refunds for the remaining days of a cancelled monthly subscription beyond the initial 48-hour window.

Annual Subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are eligible for the 48-hour money-back guarantee. After 48 hours, annual subscriptions are non-refundable. You may cancel to stop auto-renewal at the end of the annual period.

Exceptions

We reserve the right to deny refund requests if there is evidence of abuse of the refund policy (e.g., requesting refunds repeatedly, redistributing signals, or fraud).
